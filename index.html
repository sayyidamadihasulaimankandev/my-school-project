INTRODUCTION TO WEB DEVELOPMENT

Definition and function of web development

We are going to learn about the meaning of web development and its function in daily life. Web development is an important skill to possess in today's world. Web development has taken over everything we see in this digitalized era from development of websites to web applications as well as AI and Machine learning. Therefore having these skills can help a person to strive in this fast growing technological era.


In Ghana A web developer can build applications for companies like MTN, Stanbic bank etc Aside these, a web developer can also train young people who are interested in IT to enhance their digital skills. By that, web developers help to train the next generation of people to allow them make a positive impact to the society.


One impact they can make is developing programs that can help to provide solutions to real world problems like the problem of sanitation in our society.
As a web developer, I am equipped with the skills needed not only to build apps and websites but to also fix problems in society and make a positive impact in my society.
